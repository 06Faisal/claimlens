## 1 | Pre-Existing Diseases Waiting Period
The waiting period for pre-existing diseases cannot exceed 36 months of continuous coverage. Any longer period in a policy wording is overridden.

## 2 | Moratorium
After 60 months of continuous coverage no claim may be contested on grounds of non-disclosure, except for proven fraud.
