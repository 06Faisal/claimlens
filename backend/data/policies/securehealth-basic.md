# securehealth-basic | SecureHealth Basic (synthetic sample policy) | effective: 2022-10-01 | kind: policy

## 2.1 | Sum Insured
The Sum Insured under this policy is Rs. 3,00,000 per policy year.

## 3.1 | Room Rent
Room rent is limited to Rs. 3,000 per day. Associated medical expenses are reduced proportionately when a costlier room is occupied.

## 3.2 | Co-payment
The insured bears a co-payment of 10% of every admissible claim.

## 3.3 | Deductible
A deductible of Rs. 5,000 applies to each claim before the co-payment is calculated.

## 3.4 | Procedure Sub-limits
Cataract surgery is payable up to Rs. 40,000 per eye. Maternity expenses are not covered.

## 4.1 | Specific Waiting Period
Cataract and hernia are covered only after 24 months of continuous coverage.

## 4.2 | Pre-Existing Diseases
Pre-existing diseases are covered only after 48 months of continuous coverage.

## 5.1 | Exclusions
Cosmetic surgery, infertility treatment and maternity are excluded from cover.

## 5.2 | Restoration Benefit
Restoration of the Sum Insured is available once per policy year after exhaustion.
