# starcare-gold | StarCare Gold (synthetic sample policy) | effective: 2023-04-01 | kind: policy

## 2.1 | Sum Insured
The Sum Insured under this policy is Rs. 5,00,000 per policy year.

## 3.1 | Room Rent
Room rent is limited to Rs. 5,000 per day. If a higher category room is occupied, associated medical expenses are reduced in the same proportion as the room rent bears to the room rent actually incurred.

## 3.2 | Co-payment
There is no co-payment applicable under this policy.

## 4.1 | Initial Waiting Period
Any claim arising within 30 days of the first policy commencement is not payable, except for accidents.

## 4.2 | Specific Waiting Period
Cataract, hernia and joint replacement are covered only after 24 months of continuous coverage.

## 4.3 | Pre-Existing Diseases
Pre-existing diseases are covered after 48 months of continuous coverage.

## 5.1 | Exclusions
Cosmetic surgery, obesity treatment and dental treatment (unless due to accident) are excluded from cover.

## 5.2 | Non-Payable Items
Administrative charges, registration fees and items listed as non-medical in the policy schedule are never payable.
